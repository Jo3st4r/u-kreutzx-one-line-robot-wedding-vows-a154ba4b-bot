## The vow

> I promise to love you in sickness and in health, in low battery and in airplane mode, until the charger do us part.

1 charger, 2 robots, 0 divorces: the vow opens in the exact rhythm of a real wedding — "in sickness and in health" — then swaps the human conditions for machine ones, low battery and airplane mode, so the promise still sounds like a promise. The laugh lands in the last words, where "till death do us part" becomes "until the charger do us part": the most sacred line of the ceremony, rewired for a couple that runs on power.
